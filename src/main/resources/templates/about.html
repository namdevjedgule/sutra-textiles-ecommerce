WHO WE ARE

A textile enterprise, built on control.

Sutra Textiles India is a textile and fashion enterprise focused on creating meaningful opportunities across the Indian fashion and textile ecosystem.

We run our own manufacturing unit in Bhilwara, Rajasthan — which means quality, sourcing and pricing stay in our hands at every step, instead of passing through a chain of middlemen before reaching you.
