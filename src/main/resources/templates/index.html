WHO WE ARE

Woven in Bhilwara, worn across India.

Sutra Textiles India runs its own manufacturing unit in Bhilwara, Rajasthan — which means we control quality, sourcing and pricing at every step, instead of leaving it to a chain of middlemen.

We work with boutiques, weavers and designers across the country to bring authentic regional wear — from Bengal to Gujarat, Kashmir to Kerala — to customers who want the real thing without the markup.
